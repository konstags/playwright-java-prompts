User Story: SauceDemo login flow

As a user, I want to log in to the SauceDemo application so that I can see the products page.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. Open the SauceDemo login page.
2. Enter the username standard_user.
3. Move to the password field.
4. Enter the password secret_sauce.
5. Click the login control to submit the form.
6. Confirm the Products page appears after login.
